# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 18, 2026

Dart Nexus Lab ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the **POS Hub** mobile application (the "App"). 

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

### A. Personal Data
When you register or interact with the App, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information, such as:
- **Contact Information:** Name, email address, phone number.
- **Account Data:** Username and password.
- **Business Data:** Information related to your store, inventory, customers, and sales data entered into the POS system.

### B. Device and Usage Data
We may automatically collect information when you use the App, including:
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, MAC address), and IP address.
- **Usage Data:** Diagnostic data, crash reports, and performance metrics collected through third-party services (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics).

### C. Permissions Required
To provide certain features, the App may request access to the following hardware features and permissions on your device:
- **Camera:** Used for scanning QR codes and barcodes for inventory and sales.
- **Microphone (Record Audio):** Used for voice input or related features.
- **Storage (Read/Write External Storage & Media):** Used to save or retrieve local files, receipts, and images.
- **Location:** Used optionally for region-based settings, country detection, or tax calculations.
- **Biometrics:** Used for secure, local authentication to access the App.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, operate, and maintain the App.
- Process transactions and send related information, including receipts and invoices.
- Manage your account and provide customer support.
- Improve the App's functionality, performance, and user experience.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. We use the following services:
- **Google Play Services:** For core Android functionality.
- **Firebase (Google):** For authentication, cloud database storage (Firestore), crash reporting (Crashlytics), and analytics. We store your business and account data securely on Firebase servers. 

Please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information on how Google handles your data.

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. We use industry-standard encryption for data transmitted between your device and our cloud servers (Firebase). However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We will retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. 
If you wish to delete your account or request the removal of your data, you can do so by contacting us directly. Upon request, we will securely delete your account and associated business data from our servers.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under age 13, we will take steps to delete that information.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
