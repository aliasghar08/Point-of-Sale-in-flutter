# Data Deletion Instructions

**POS Hub** is committed to your privacy and gives you full control over your data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, you can do so by following the instructions below.

## How to Delete Your Account and Data

### Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)
1. Open the **POS Hub** app on your device.
2. Ensure you are logged into the account you wish to delete.
3. Navigate to the **Settings** or **Profile** section of the app.
4. Locate and tap the **"Delete Account"** or **"Delete Data"** option.
5. Confirm your choice when prompted. Your account and all associated business data will be permanently deleted from our servers.

### Method 2: Request via Email
If you no longer have access to the app or prefer we handle it for you, you can request manual data deletion:
1. Send an email to **support@dartnexuslab.com**.
2. Subject line: **"Account Deletion Request"**.
3. In the body of the email, please include the email address associated with your POS Hub account.
4. We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 7 business days. We will reply to your email to confirm once the deletion is complete.

## What Data is Deleted?
When you request account deletion, we will permanently remove:
- Your personal profile information (Name, Email, etc.).
- All business, inventory, and sales data associated with your account in our cloud database.
- Your authentication credentials.

*Note: Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.*

If you have any further questions, please contact us at support@dartnexuslab.com.
